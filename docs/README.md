# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through initiation, planning, iterative execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation validates the business need, success measures, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resources; planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, owners, milestones, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams track work on a project board, deliver small increments, and review progress through demos and delivery syncs.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, dependencies, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team and stakeholder updates keep work aligned, with blockers escalated from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed. Quality practices include unit and integration testing, CI tests and linting, security scanning, critical-flow smoke tests, and documented release verification and rollback plans. Retrospectives turn lessons into owned, time-bound improvement actions.
